Who We Are

About Our Organization

We are a dedicated community of educators, parents, and advocates committed to providing the highest quality resources for homeschooling families.

Since our founding, we have focused on creating an inclusive, supportive environment where children can thrive academically and socially. We believe that community-driven education builds stronger families and brighter futures for everyone involved.

Community Led Initiative

Built from the ground up by parents, for parents.

Our Purpose

Our Mission

"To empower parents with the tools, community, and support necessary to deliver a world-class, personalized education right from home."

We strive to break down the barriers of traditional schooling by offering accessible, high-quality collaborative resources. Every program, workshop, and field trip is designed with the singular goal of enriching the home education experience for all.

Our Journey

Our History

A straightforward path of growth, dedication, and community building since day one.

2018

The Foundation

Started by three homeschooling families in a local community center, our co-op began as a humble book-sharing club. We quickly realized the immense need for collaborative educational spaces and began formalizing our organization.

2021

Expanding Horizons

As membership rapidly grew to over 100 families, we moved into our first official facility. This critical milestone allowed us to finally introduce dedicated STEM labs, structured art studios, and a comprehensive shared library system.

2026

A Regional Hub

Today, we proudly support over 500 active families. Our state-of-the-art resource center stands as a beacon for alternative education in the state, offering unmatched support, community programs, and lifelong connections.
