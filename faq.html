Getting Started

Membership & Enrollment

Everything you need to know about joining and maintaining your co-op membership.

1. How do I enroll my child in the Co-Op?

Enrolling is simple! First, create an account on our Parent Portal. From there, you can choose your desired membership tier, submit the necessary family information, and schedule your initial onboarding tour of the facility.

2. Are there any hidden fees or extra costs?

No hidden fees. Your monthly membership covers facility usage, basic shared library access, and parent workshops. However, specific intensive courses (like the Summer STEM Bootcamp) or premium field trips may require a separate, optional enrollment fee.

3. Can I pause or cancel my membership?

Yes. We understand that family circumstances change. You can pause or cancel your standard monthly membership at any time through your dashboard. Please note that Annual Passes are non-refundable after the first 30 days.

4. Do you offer scholarships or financial aid?

We firmly believe education should be accessible to all. We offer a limited number of need-based scholarships each semester. Please contact our admissions team directly to request an application form.

5. What age groups do you accommodate?

Our core programs are designed for children aged 5 through 18. We have specific modules broken down by developmental stages: Early Learners (5-7), Juniors (8-11), Middle Grades (12-14), and High School (15-18).

6. Can I switch membership tiers in the middle of the year?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your membership tier at the start of any billing cycle. Any upgrades will be prorated, allowing you immediate access to higher-tier benefits like the STEM labs or extended tutoring.

Daily Operations

Facilities & Curriculum

Insights on how our physical space and educational programs operate.

1. What are your facility operating hours?

Our resource center is open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Special weekend events or parent workshops may occasionally extend these hours, which will be posted on the community calendar.

2. Are parents required to stay on-site during classes?

For children under the age of 12, a parent or designated guardian must remain on the premises in one of our parent lounges or coworking spaces. Students 12 and older may be dropped off for their registered class blocks.

3. How do you handle emergency and medical situations?

Safety is our priority. We have CPR and First Aid-certified staff on duty at all times. All parents must fill out comprehensive medical release forms upon enrollment detailing allergies, conditions, and emergency contacts.

4. Do you provide a standardized curriculum?

We do not enforce a single curriculum. Parents remain the primary educators and choose the curriculum that works best for their child. Our group classes and labs act as supplemental, enriching experiences to complement your chosen curriculum at home.

5. How does the shared library system work?

Our digital portal acts like a public library system. You can browse available textbooks, microscopes, board games, and art supplies, reserve them online, and pick them up at the front desk. Checkout limits depend on your membership tier.

6. Are the tutors and parent-instructors background-checked?

Yes. Every tutor, instructor, and staff member undergoes a thorough state and federal background check before they are allowed to host classes, run lab sessions, or mentor students within our facility.
